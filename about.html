About us

A little taste of home.

Burmese Kitchen is a home-based e-business idea providing authentic Burmese homemade food in Bangkok.

  • The problem: Busy customers may not have enough time to cook, while authentic Burmese food can be difficult to find.
  • Our solution: Simple online ordering through our website and social channels.
  • Fulfilment: Customers can choose delivery or pickup.
  • Payments: PromptPay, bank transfer, card and e-wallet options.
